## Part 2: YouTube Script

---

**VIDEO TITLE:** Level 1 Reflection: Turn Twelve Weeks of Learning Into Real Change | Chanma Investment Tutorial

**RUNTIME TARGET:** ~5 minutes

**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail investor, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO]**

[VISUAL: Title card "Level 1 Reflection: From Knowledge to Action"]

**Horace:** Welcome back. We have just finished twelve weeks of foundation. Before we move to Level 2, I want to slow down and ask the most important question in this whole course.

**Stella:** Which is?

**Horace:** What are you actually going to do about it? Knowledge that does not change behavior is decoration. It feels good to know, but it does not move your life.

**Stella:** That is a little uncomfortable. I have been taking notes, but I have not opened a brokerage account yet.

**Horace:** And you are not alone. Most people who finish a course like this never act on it. The point of this reflection is to make sure you are not one of them.

---

**[SEGMENT 1: HONEST QUESTIONS]**

**Horace:** Sit with a few questions before you move on. How much of your money is sitting in a checking account right now? In dollars. Not "some". Not "a little". The actual number.

**Stella:** Probably more than I would admit out loud.

**Horace:** That is the point. Inflation is taxing that idle cash every single day. If you walk away from Level 1 with nothing but the willingness to look at that number -- and do something about it -- you have already won.

**Stella:** What about behavioral biases? I remember the lesson, but I am not sure which one is mine.

**Horace:** Pick the one that stings a little when you read it. Loss aversion, herding, recency, overconfidence. The one you do not want to admit to is usually the right one.

---

**[SEGMENT 2: ACTIONS THIS WEEK]**

**Stella:** OK so beyond reflecting, what should viewers do this week?

**Horace:** Pick two. Open a brokerage account if you have not. Buy your first ETF, even with a small amount. Move your idle savings into a money market fund. Pull a real 10-K and read one page. Write down your target asset allocation on paper.

**Stella:** Why two specifically?

**Horace:** Because one is too easy to skip and five is too easy to delay forever. Two is the right amount of friction to actually move.

---

**[SEGMENT 3: BEHAVIOR CHANGE]**

**Stella:** This feels less like an investing lesson and more like a life lesson.

**Horace:** That is exactly what it is. The hardest part of investing is not picking stocks -- it is changing the beliefs you walked in with. "I will start when I have more money." "The market is too risky." "I will let someone else handle it." If those beliefs survive Level 1, the rest of the course will not save you.

**Stella:** And if I commit to changing them?

**Horace:** Then write it down. One sentence. Specific. Dated. "By Sunday I will open an account and put $500 into VOO." Something you can tick off and prove. Vague intentions evaporate. Specific commitments compound.

---

**[OUTRO]**

**Stella:** I am going to do mine tonight.

**Horace:** Good. When you have done it, come back for Level 2. From here forward, the course assumes Level 1 is no longer theory -- it is your life.

**[END]**
