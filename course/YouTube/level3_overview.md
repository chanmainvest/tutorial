**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail trader, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO]**

[VISUAL: Title card "Level 3: Advanced - Weeks 25-36"]

**Horace:** Welcome to Level 3. This is where many investors either take a major leap forward or get lost. We are covering options and fixed income -- two areas that sound intimidating but are actually some of the most practical tools available.

**Stella:** I will be honest, I am nervous about options. Every time I hear about them, it is either someone who made a fortune or someone who lost everything.

**Horace:** That is because most people encounter options through speculation stories. We are going to approach them completely differently. In Week 25, we start with the basics, and by Week 26, I am going to change how you think about options entirely -- we will show you that they are really just enhanced versions of orders you already use.

**Stella:** What do you mean?

**Horace:** A cash-secured put is essentially a limit buy order where you get paid to wait. A covered call is like a limit sell order where you get paid to wait. Once you see options through that lens, the fear disappears.

**Stella:** And then we get into the income strategies?

**Horace:** Weeks 27 and 28 cover covered calls and cash-secured puts -- the bread and butter income strategies. Week 29 introduces the Greeks, which tell you exactly how your options will behave. And Week 30 covers credit spreads and iron condors for defined-risk income.

**Stella:** What about the fixed income half?

**Horace:** Weeks 31 through 36 are equally powerful. You will learn to read yield curves like an economist, understand duration and convexity for managing rate risk, evaluate credit quality, and by Week 36, you will build a complete income-generating portfolio that combines everything -- dividends, bonds, options, and REITs.

**Stella:** That sounds like a portfolio that actually pays you every month.

**Horace:** That is exactly the goal. Let us get into it.

**[END]**
