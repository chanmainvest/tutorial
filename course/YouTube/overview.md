## Part 2: YouTube Script

---

**VIDEO TITLE:** Chanma Investment Tutorial: From Beginner to Professional | Course Overview

**RUNTIME TARGET:** ~5 minutes

**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail trader, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO SEQUENCE]**

[VISUAL: Title card with "Chanma Investment Tutorial" and tagline "From Beginner to Professional"]

**Horace:** Welcome. I am Horace, and this is the Chanma Investment Tutorial -- a free, 52-week course that will take you from knowing nothing about investing to managing a professional-grade portfolio.

**Stella:** And I am Stella. I just graduated from college, I have some savings sitting in a bank account, and I know I should be doing something smarter with them. But honestly, every time I try to learn about investing, I get overwhelmed by jargon and conflicting advice.

**Horace:** That is exactly why this course exists. I have spent years studying the markets -- CFA curriculum, decades of trading experience, countless books and research papers. At some point I realized that all of my notes, if organized properly, could be a complete investment education. So that is what we built.

**Stella:** Who is this course for?

**Horace:** Anyone who wants to understand investing properly. Complete beginners start at Level 1 and build up from there. But even if you have been investing for a while, I would encourage you to go through the early levels -- you might be surprised at the gaps in your foundation.

---

**[SEGMENT 1: COURSE STRUCTURE]**

[VISUAL: Pyramid diagram showing 5 levels stacking from Foundation to Expert]

**Horace:** The course is organized into five levels across 52 weeks. Think of it as a pyramid.

**Stella:** Walk me through the levels.

**Horace:** Level 1 is Foundation -- twelve weeks covering why investing matters, index funds, risk and return, bonds, gold, what stocks and equity actually are, financial statements, behavioural biases and rebalancing, and building your first portfolio. By Week 12, you will have a real, diversified portfolio up and running.

**Stella:** And that is just the beginning?

**Horace:** Level 2 is Intermediate -- you learn long and short trading, multi-asset allocation, sector rotation, performance metrics, equity valuation, and factor investing. By Week 24, you are thinking like a serious investor.

**Stella:** Then options?

**Horace:** Level 3 covers options and fixed income. But we do not teach options as speculation. We teach them as enhanced order types -- covered calls for income, cash-secured puts for entry, credit spreads for defined-risk trades. Plus a deep dive into bonds, yield curves, and credit analysis.

**Stella:** What about Level 4?

**Horace:** That is where it gets sophisticated -- options leverage, LEAPS, futures, VIX trading, professional risk management, backtesting. The tools that separate casual investors from serious ones.

**Stella:** And Level 5 is the capstone?

**Horace:** Six weeks where we bring everything together. Tail risk hedging, structured products, volatility arbitrage, managed futures, and the final integration -- building your all-weather portfolio.

---

**[SEGMENT 2: WHAT MAKES THIS COURSE DIFFERENT]**

**Stella:** There are a lot of investment courses out there. What makes this one different?

**Horace:** Three things. First, it is structured progressively. Every week builds on the previous one. You do not jump into options without understanding risk. You do not learn factor investing without understanding what factors are.

**Stella:** Second?

**Horace:** It is based on real curriculum. The CFA program is the gold standard for investment professionals. We have taken that body of knowledge and reorganized it for retail investors -- keeping the rigor but cutting the irrelevant parts.

**Stella:** And third?

**Horace:** It is free. MIT License. Use it, share it, build on it. The notes were already written, the research was already done. There is no reason to lock it behind a paywall.

---

**[SEGMENT 3: HOW TO USE THIS COURSE]**

**Stella:** How should people approach this?

**Horace:** One lesson per week. The article and the video cover the same material in two different formats -- read the article first, watch the video first, or just pick one and skip the other. Whichever way you absorb it best is the right way. The only thing I care about is that you walk away understanding the concept. We also have 30 side lessons on practical topics like calculator usage, tax efficiency, retirement accounts, and specific strategies. Take those whenever you need them.

**Stella:** And if people have questions?

**Horace:** Two ways. First, the course website has an AI tutor built right into every page -- a small Gemma model that runs entirely in your own browser, knows the lesson you are reading, and can answer follow-up questions instantly. Use that for the on-the-spot stuff: clarifications, definitions, "wait, what did he just mean by that." For the deeper conversations -- where I want to actually talk back, share what I am positioning in real markets, and answer the questions that need a human -- come find us on Patreon. That is where the real Q&A happens.

**Stella:** I am excited to get started. Let us jump into Week 1.

**Horace:** See you there.
