## Part 2: YouTube Script

---

**VIDEO TITLE:** Level 2 Reflection: From Active to Disciplined | Chanma Investment Tutorial

**RUNTIME TARGET:** ~5 minutes

**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail investor, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO]**

[VISUAL: Title card "Level 2 Reflection: Becoming a Decision-Maker"]

**Horace:** Welcome back. Twelve more weeks behind us. Before we step into options and fixed income in Level 3, I want to ask you the same uncomfortable question I asked at the end of Level 1.

**Stella:** What are you actually doing about it?

**Horace:** Exactly. But this time it is sharper. Level 2 turned you into someone who makes decisions -- short, long, allocation, valuation, factor bets. So the question becomes: are you making *good* decisions, or just *more* decisions?

**Stella:** I have made a few trades since Level 1. I am honestly not sure I could defend all of them.

**Horace:** Most active investors cannot. That is not an indictment, it is the starting point.

---

**[SEGMENT 1: HONEST QUESTIONS]**

**Horace:** Ask yourself why you are active at all. Real edge or boredom? Boredom is the most expensive feeling in finance.

**Stella:** And the metrics question?

**Horace:** Can you state your portfolio's Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, and volatility? In actual numbers? If not, you are operating an aircraft with no instruments.

**Stella:** That hits.

**Horace:** It is supposed to. Knowing the numbers is not optional from this level forward.

---

**[SEGMENT 2: ACTIONS THIS MONTH]**

**Stella:** What should viewers go and do?

**Horace:** Pick three. Compute your Sharpe. Add one non-US asset class. Run a real valuation on something you already own. Pick a factor tilt you can defend. Write down your sector view in one sentence.

**Stella:** And finish them within the month?

**Horace:** Within the month. Not "eventually". Eventually is where good intentions go to die.

---

**[SEGMENT 3: BEHAVIOR CHANGE]**

**Stella:** What about the mindset shifts?

**Horace:** Three big ones. Stop trading for the sake of trading. Separate good decisions from good outcomes -- they are not the same thing. And resist stacking strategies you cannot monitor. One strategy you actually execute beats five strategies you only read about.

**Stella:** And the valuation traps?

**Horace:** Now that you have models, you can torture them. Watch for that. Try to disprove your thesis before you commit money to it. If you cannot find a counterargument, you have not looked hard enough.

---

**[OUTRO]**

**Stella:** Level 3 is options and bonds. Should I be nervous?

**Horace:** Only if you carry sloppy decision-making into precision tools. Tighten your process now. Write your commitment. Then we begin.

**[END]**
