## Part 2: YouTube Script

---

**VIDEO TITLE:** Level 1 Overview: Your Foundation in Investing | Chanma Investment Tutorial

**RUNTIME TARGET:** ~5 minutes

**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail trader, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO]**

[VISUAL: Title card "Level 1: Foundation - Your First 12 Weeks"]

**Horace:** Welcome to Level 1 of the Chanma Investment Tutorial. I am Horace, and over the next twelve weeks, we are going to build the foundation that everything else in this course rests on.

**Stella:** And I am Stella. I am starting from zero here -- I just graduated, I have some savings, and I know I should be investing, but honestly I do not really know where to begin.

**Horace:** That is exactly where we want you. Level 1 assumes no prior knowledge. We start with the most important question in finance: why should you invest at all?

**Stella:** That is Week 1, right? The time value of money?

**Horace:** Exactly. We will show you why money sitting in a bank account is actually losing value every single day due to inflation. Then in Weeks 2 through 4, we introduce the simplest and most effective tools -- index funds and ETFs, the tradeoff between risk and return, and the classic 60/40 portfolio.

**Stella:** And then it gets more interesting?

**Horace:** Weeks 5 through 8 expand your toolkit. You will learn how bonds act as a shock absorber in your portfolio, why a small permanent gold sleeve has reduced portfolio volatility across multiple historical regimes, what stock and equity actually mean as a fractional ownership claim on a real business, and how to read the three core financial statements -- income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement.

**Stella:** That last one sounds intimidating.

**Horace:** It is not as hard as it looks once you know what to focus on. Then in Weeks 9 through 12, we put it all together. You will understand how market indexes work, how economic cycles affect your investments, the behavioral biases that cause most investors to buy high and sell low — and the rebalancing rules that mechanically force you to do the right trade when your brain wants to do the wrong one — and finally, you will build your first real portfolio.

**Stella:** So by Week 12, I will actually have a portfolio?

**Horace:** A properly diversified one, built on principles you understand. That is the whole point -- not just following instructions, but understanding the reasoning behind every decision.

**Stella:** I am ready. Let us get started.

**Horace:** See you in Week 1.

**[END]**
