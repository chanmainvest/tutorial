## Part 2: YouTube Script

---

**VIDEO TITLE:** Level 2 Overview: Becoming a Serious Investor | Chanma Investment Tutorial

**RUNTIME TARGET:** ~5 minutes

**HOSTS:**
- **Horace** (teacher): Experienced retail trader, explains concepts from years of market experience
- **Stella** (student): Recent college graduate learning to invest her savings, asks the questions viewers are thinking

---

**[INTRO]**

[VISUAL: Title card "Level 2: Intermediate - Weeks 13-24"]

**Horace:** Welcome to Level 2. If you completed Level 1, you already have a solid portfolio and understand the basics. Now we are going to level up.

**Stella:** I have my 60/40 portfolio running. It feels good, but I keep hearing about all these other strategies. Am I missing out?

**Horace:** That is exactly what Level 2 addresses. We start with some concepts that open up new possibilities -- like short selling. In Week 13, you will learn that you can profit when prices go down, not just when they go up.

**Stella:** Short selling always sounded risky and complicated.

**Horace:** It can be, but understanding it is essential even if you never do it yourself. It is how hedging works, and it is the basis for pair trading, which we cover in Week 14. Then Weeks 15 and 16 take your portfolio global with multi-asset allocation and sector rotation.

**Stella:** What about the analysis side?

**Horace:** Weeks 17 through 20 are where you learn to look under the hood. We cover performance metrics -- because raw returns do not tell the whole story. A 15% return with wild swings is very different from a 15% return with steady growth. Then we tackle interest rates, corporate finance, and earnings analysis.

**Stella:** And the last four weeks?

**Horace:** That is where it all comes together. You will learn three different approaches to stock valuation, understand how currency affects your international investments, discover factor investing, and then build a multi-strategy portfolio that combines everything.

**Stella:** So by Week 24, I will have a much more sophisticated setup?

**Horace:** You will have the tools and knowledge to construct a portfolio that a professional would respect. Let us get started.

**[END]**
